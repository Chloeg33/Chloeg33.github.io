Academic Vocabulary Assessment 02 (20 points)

Specialized terminology for academic proficiency

1. The term stress is ___________ to stimuli or events in our environment that make physical and emotional demands on us.

2. Many sorts of events can be __________, including disasters, major life events, and daily hassles.

3. When stressors _________ with our accustomed way of life, we must deal with them.

4. How well we succeed in doing so will _________ how serious a toll stress will take on our well-being.

5. Seyle proposed that humans react to any stressor in three stages, known as the general _________ syndrome.

6. The first stage of the general adaptation syndrome is the __________ reaction, where the body becomes highly alert.

7. After the alarm reaction, the organism enters the stage of __________, trying to adapt to the stress.

8. If the organism cannot adapt, it enters a stage of __________, or collapse.

9. The specific stress reaction is likely to ________ widely; some give rise to anger, and some to helplessness.

10. An important influence on people's ability to ________ with stressful situations is the degree of control they feel.

11. Sense of control can help ________ the negative consequences of stress, both psychological and physical.

12. Even when you cannot control them, unpleasant events tend to be less stressful if they are ________.

13. Less stress occurs if predictable events ________; this was demonstrated by Weiss in another study with rats.

14. Stress reactions can reduce the disease-fighting ________ of the body's immune system, increasing susceptibility to illnesses.

15. Stress tends to be the ________ in stress-related illnesses such as stomach ulcers.

16. Stress leads to increased ________ of hydrochloric acid in the stomach.

17. Stress and stress reactions can cause the disease-fighting effectiveness of the body's immune system, thereby increasing __________ to illnesses, ranging from colds to cancer.

18. The field of research on psychological factors influencing immune function is called ________.

19. Ulcers are a common disorder among people who work in occupations that make heavy psychological ________, from assembly-line workers to air-traffic controllers.

20. Medical scientists and researchers are still trying to understand the biological ________ of cell behavior that underlie the onset and development of cancer.
