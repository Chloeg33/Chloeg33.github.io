Academic Vocabulary Quiz 01 (30 points)

Choose the best word to complete each sentence.

1. The term stress is ___________ to stimuli or events in our environment that make physical and emotional demands on us.

2. Many sorts of events can be __________, including disasters, major life events, and daily hassles.

3. When stressors _________ with our accustomed way of life, we must deal with them.

4. How well we succeed in doing so will _________ how serious a toll stress will take on our well-being.

5. Seyle proposed that humans react to any stressor in three stages, known as the general _________ syndrome.

6. The first stage of the general adaptation syndrome is the __________ reaction, where the body becomes highly alert.

7. After the alarm reaction, the organism enters the stage of __________, trying to adapt to the stress.

8. If the organism cannot adapt, it enters a stage of __________, or collapse.

9. Whether a particular ________ will be stressful depends on the person's subjective appraisal of that stimulus.

10. The specific stress reaction is likely to ________ widely; some give rise to anger, and some to helplessness.

11. One of the lawyers finds the situation extremely stressful; she experiences headaches and stomach upset, and has difficulty ________.

12. An important influence on people's ability to ________ with stressful situations is the degree of control they feel.

13. Sense of control can help ________ the negative consequences of stress, both psychological and physical.

14. Even when you cannot control them, unpleasant events tend to be less stressful if they are ________.

15. Less stress occurs if predictable events ________; this was demonstrated by Weiss in another study with rats.

16. Stress reactions can reduce the disease-fighting ________ of the body's immune system, increasing susceptibility to illnesses.

17. Stress tends to be the ________ in stress-related illnesses such as stomach ulcers.

18. Stress leads to increased ________ of hydrochloric acid in the stomach.

19. Researchers are still trying to understand the biological mechanisms that ________ the onset and development of cancer.

20. The field of research on psychological factors influencing immune function is called ________.

21. It is Friday evening and two young lawyers get phone calls at home. The trial date for an important case has been moved up. Both of the lawyers will now have to prepare a report for the case by Monday morning. It is a __________ situation for both.

22. One of the lawyers finds the situation extremely _________; she feels tremendous anxiety.

23. Both animals and humans have been found to cope better with ________ or threatening stimuli when they feel they can exercise some degree of control.

24. In one well-known experiment, one pair of rats was given a degree of control over the situation; it could reach through a hole in the cage and press a panel that would turn off the shock both for itself and for its partner. Thus, one was ________ and helped, and the other was in control.

25. In many stressful situations, the body's __________ can improve our performance – we become more energetic, more alert, and better able to take effective action.

26. As we will be seen later in this unit, the body's reactions are more likely to be ________ than helpful to us when stress is encountered continually.

27. Stress reactions can reduce the disease-fighting ________ of the body's immune system, thereby increasing susceptibility to illnesses.

28. Stress and stress reactions can cause the disease-fighting effectiveness of the body's immune system, thereby increasing __________ to illnesses, ranging from colds to cancer.

29. Ulcers are a common disorder among people who work in occupations that make heavy psychological ________, from assembly-line workers to air-traffic controllers.

30. Medical scientists and researchers are still trying to understand the biological ________ of cell behavior that underlie the onset and development of cancer.
